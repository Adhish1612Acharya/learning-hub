## In my words

Dale Carnegie says criticising people is of no use. It's human nature: they will either criticise you back or say they didn't know how they could have done better, but they never accept it. So criticising people is not effective.

One example I remember: an engineer authoritatively told the workers to put their hats on to follow the safety rules. They slowly agreed at the time, but when he left, the workers took the hats off.

So he changed his strategy. When he saw a worker not wearing a hat, he would first ask if the hat was uncomfortable, and then tell them the hat was made for their safety. This resulted in more workers following the rules.
