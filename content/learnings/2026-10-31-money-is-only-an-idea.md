## In my words

I've been reading Rich Dad Poor Dad for a while and I'm near the end of the book. Today I learned a small part of it: money is only an idea. If you want more money, simply change your thinking.

Education and wisdom about money are important. Start early, buy a book, go to a seminar, practise, start small.

I also got to know about a new book called *Think and Grow Rich*. It doesn't say work hard and grow rich, it says **think** and grow rich.

And today's lesson: don't play it safe, play it smart.
